# Ivgar

1. Once upon a time, about 300 years ago, an elf named Ingvar lived by one of Sweden’s lakes. He was only 8 centimetres tall, but he had enough energy to do the work of three!
    He helped people drain parts of the lake, where iron ore was being extracted. He knew everything about water, mud and wet, messy work.
    When the work was finished, Ingvar didn't know what to do with himself. So he built a little cottage and a tiny wooden hut in a nearby tree. Inside, he placed a book where people could write down their stories.
    And so, the former lake-dwelling helper became the keeper of human memories.
    Because even the smallest among us can preserve the biggest stories.
